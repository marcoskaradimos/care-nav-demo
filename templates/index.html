Non-Urgent Symptoms

🧍 General / Whole Body

  • Mild fatigue that's been ongoing for a few weeks
  • Unexplained mild weight changes
  • General feelings of being "run down"

🩹 Skin

  • Slow-changing moles (not bleeding or rapidly growing)
  • Mild rashes, dry skin, or eczema flare-ups
  • Acne or skin texture concerns

🫃 Digestive

  • Mild, intermittent bloating or indigestion
  • Changes in bowel habits that have been gradual (not sudden or with blood)
  • Mild nausea without vomiting

🧠 Mental Health

  • Low mood or anxiety that is manageable but affecting quality of life
  • Sleep difficulties
  • Stress-related concerns

🦴 Musculoskeletal

  • Mild joint or muscle aches not linked to injury
  • Stiffness in joints over time
  • Mild back pain without loss of bladder/bowel control

🫁 Respiratory

  • A mild cough lasting more than 3 weeks (without severe shortness of breath)
  • Ongoing mild congestion or post-nasal drip
If you are unsure whether your symptoms are urgent, please call NHS 111 for guidance.
